(04) ABOUT

Inpha One is a prog metal band from Sydney, Australia. Formed in 2019, the band's style is influenced by bands like Deftones, Northlane, Tesseract, and Tool.

The current lineup features Oleg Filippenko on vocals, Dennis McCarthy on guitar, Shane Mulligan on bass, and Jake Shaw on drums.

Since starting, Inpha One has released their debut album, In Phaneron of One, and a 2023 EP titled Lights and Shapes. They also put out a few singles in 2024, including "Wasteland," "Grey Days," and "North Star." Right now, the band is working on a new full-length album, which is planned for release in 2027.
